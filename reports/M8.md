## The S1 failure, and what caused it

Retrieval puts 23/26 facts into the top-10 context (E3); the full stack
returns 10–16/26 in answers. The measured decomposition of run B's 10 lost
facts (offline probe against the eval database, zero provider calls):

- Evidence gate — 5 facts. d07's three fact chunks score below the 0.35
  threshold (best 0.3367); d09's two facts sit in a chunk scoring 0.2209.
  The threshold was calibrated on answerable top-1 scores; facts in
  lower-ranked chunks fall through.
- Grounding validator — 4 facts. d03 and d11 were refused `ungrounded` in
  both runs with their fact chunks in the prompt (0.4521, 0.6808). The
  validator discards the whole answer over any unsupported claim; on a
  famous paper the model's memory-written claims are the likely trigger
  (refused drafts are not returned by the API, so this is inferred from
  refusal reasons).
- Partial answer — 1 fact (d12).

Fixes that stay honest (lowering the threshold to 0.30 recovers at most
3 facts; d09's chunk is unreachable) and the deeper refinements
(fact-aware gating, per-claim grounding) are recorded for Project 9 in
`DECISIONS.md`. No threshold was re-registered.

## Incidents

- **Provider 503 cluster in run A.** Five questions (d08, d10, d11, d12,
  a03) returned HTTP 503 with zero calls spent, mid-run. The failure rule
  was applied: only the affected questions were re-run (5 requests); four
  resolved. d08 returned 503 on two further attempts across ~40 minutes
  while neighbouring questions succeeded — recorded as a provider
  availability casualty; run B answers d08 2/2.
- **Run variance.** The A–B S1 difference (9 facts as originally stopped,
  6 after repair) exceeds the 2-fact band and is stated in
  `docs/EVALUATION.md` as required: provider nondeterminism on identical
  requests is larger than the band.
- **Claims-check self-reference.** The checker flagged the forbidden-phrases
  list in FACTS.md itself (it quotes them to forbid them) and the technical
  term "URL-safe". Both were fixed in the checker (ignore-regions for
  quoted policy text; "URL-safe" excluded), with tests, rather than by
  weakening the word list.
